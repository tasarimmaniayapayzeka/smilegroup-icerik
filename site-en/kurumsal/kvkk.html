Personal Data Protection (KVKK)

TR original: KVKK — KVKK Aydınlatma Metni
LEGAL · Our information notice for patients under KVKK (Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law, Law No. 6698).

Information Notice on the Protection and Processing of Personal Data

TR: Kişisel Verilerin Korunması ve İşlenmesi Aydınlatma Metni

Identity of the Data Controller

TR: Veri Sorumlusunun Kimliği

Under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK" — Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law), your personal data may be processed by [Company's Full Registered Trade Name] ("Smile Group" or the "Clinic"), in its capacity as data controller, within the scope described below.

Personal Data Processed and the Purposes of Processing

TR: İşlenen Kişisel Veriler ve İşlenme Amaçları

The following personal data are processed through our website (https://www.smilegroup.com.tr/):

Identity and Contact Data: Your first name, surname, telephone number and email address are processed for the purposes of creating your appointments, responding to your contact requests and ensuring that clinic processes run without interruption through live-support/CRM systems (e.g. Chatwoot infrastructure).

Special Categories of Personal Data (Health Data): Your medical history, dental treatment requests and examination details are processed by our dentists and authorised healthcare staff, who are bound by a duty of professional confidentiality, for the purposes of providing medical diagnosis, treatment and care services.

Transaction Security and Financial Data: Your browsing data on our website and your IP address — and, where an online payment is collected, financial information processed through payment infrastructures (PayTR, VakıfPay, etc.) — are processed for the purposes of conducting contractual processes and maintaining information security.

How Personal Data Are Collected and the Legal Basis

TR: Kişisel Verilerin Toplanma Yöntemi ve Hukuki Sebebi

Your personal data are collected electronically through the forms on our site, appointment modules, cookies and the web technologies we host (Next.js, Laravel, etc.). These data are processed on the legal grounds set out in Articles 5 and 6 of the KVKK: the establishment or performance of a contract, the fulfilment of our legal obligations, the legitimate interest of the data controller and, in the case of health data, processing by persons under a duty of confidentiality for the purpose of medical diagnosis/treatment (or, where required, on the basis of your explicit consent).

To Whom, and for What Purposes, Personal Data May Be Transferred

TR: İşlenen Kişisel Verilerin Kimlere ve Hangi Amaçla Aktarılabileceği

With the necessary security measures in place, your personal data may be transferred:

  • to the Ministry of Health, Provincial Health Directorates and the relevant public authorities, as required by our legal obligations;
  • to the domestic server (VPS, Hostinger and similar hosting services) and database providers that keep our website running quickly and securely;
  • and, where financial obligations are being fulfilled, to our contracted payment institutions and our certified public accountants.

Your Rights under the KVKK

TR: KVKK Kapsamındaki Haklarınız

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your personal data are being processed, to know whether they are being used in line with their purpose, to know the third parties within Turkey to whom they are transferred, and — where they have been processed incompletely or inaccurately — to request that they be corrected, deleted or destroyed. You may submit your requests in writing to [Company's Registered Address], or with your secure electronic signature to [KEP (registered electronic mail) address or kvkk@smilegroup.com.tr].
