A “New You” with Smile Group

An Approach That Adds Value to Your Smile

TR: Gülüşünüze Değer Katan Bir Yaklaşım

Watch the clinic tour (video)

At Smile Group, we bring together cosmetic dentistry, implant treatments and digital dentistry with an approach built around planning for the individual. We listen to each patient's needs, assess the treatment process together, and plan every step with clear, open communication.

By pairing modern technology with the approach of our experienced clinical team, we aim to offer a treatment experience that is comfortable, predictable and reassuring.

  • Digital Planning — Your treatment is planned digitally before it ever begins.
  • A Personalised Approach — A treatment plan is built around each patient's individual needs.
  • Open Communication — Every stage of the process is shared clearly and plainly.
  • Up-to-Date Treatment Technology — Treatments supported by modern equipment and a digital infrastructure.
  • International Patient Experience — Planned, coordinated care for patients travelling from abroad.
  • Aftercare & Follow-Up — Communication and support continue well after your treatment ends.

Knowledge Bank & Guides

Frequently Asked Questions

TR: Sıkça Sorulan Sorular

Everything about health tourism, travel and treatments.

01 · How many days does smile design treatment take in total?

Smile design usually takes just 5 to 6 days. On the first day, digital intraoral scans and a try-in of the restoration are carried out. Within 2–3 days your porcelain or zirconia crowns are produced in our laboratory, and on day 5 the final comfortable try-in and bonding are completed.
Clinic-approved information · Topic: Treatments

02 · How can I get a free treatment plan without visiting the clinic?

Use the X-ray and photo upload area on our homepage to send us your panoramic X-ray or clear photos of your smile taken on a phone. Our lead clinicians prepare a personalised quotation within 24 hours.
Clinic-approved information · Topic: Travel

03 · Are flights, a 5-star hotel and VIP transfers included in the treatment package?

Yes! For all our international patients above a certain treatment threshold, luxury accommodation in a 5-star hotel, an airport welcome and VIP Mercedes transfers between the hotel and the clinic are provided with our compliments.
Clinic-approved information · Topic: Travel

04 · What does the lifetime warranty cover if a crown fractures?

All Straumann titanium implants placed at our clinic carry a lifetime warranty certificate that is valid worldwide. Our zirconia and porcelain crowns are covered by a 7-year clinical warranty.
Clinic-approved information · Topic: General

05 · Is treatment painful? Will I need sedation or anaesthesia?

Because every procedure is carried out under local anaesthetic, you won't feel pain during treatment. For patients who feel anxious about treatment, laughing-gas sedation is also offered at no extra charge.
Clinic-approved information · Topic: Treatments
