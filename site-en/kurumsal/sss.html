Frequently Asked Questions

TR original: Sıkça Sorulan Sorular
Answers to all the questions you may have about health tourism, treatment processes and our clinic.

01How many days does smile design treatment take in total?

TR: Gülüş tasarımı tedavisi toplamda kaç gün sürer?

Smile design usually takes just five to six days. On the first day, digital intraoral scans and a trial fitting of the prosthesis are carried out. Within two to three days your porcelain or zirconia crowns are produced in our laboratory, and on day five the final, comfortable try-in and bonding are completed.

Clinic-verified information · Topic: Treatments

02How can I get a free treatment plan without visiting the clinic?

TR: Klinik ziyareti gerçekleştirmeden nasıl ücretsiz tedavi planı alabilirim?

Use the X-ray and photo upload area on our homepage to send us your panoramic X-ray or clear smile photos taken on your phone. Our chief dentists will prepare a personalised quotation for you within 24 hours.

Clinic-verified information · Topic: Travel

03Are flights, a five-star hotel and VIP transfers included in the treatment package?

TR: Uçak, 5 yıldızlı otel ve VIP transferler tedavi paketine dahil midir?

Yes! For all our international patients whose treatment is above a certain value, luxury accommodation in a five-star hotel, an airport welcome and VIP Mercedes transfers between the hotel and the clinic are our complimentary gift.

Clinic-verified information · Topic: Travel

04How does the lifetime guarantee work if a crown breaks?

TR: Kaplamanın kırılması durumunda ömür boyu garanti kapsamı nasıldır?

All Straumann titanium implants placed at our clinic are certified with a lifetime guarantee that is valid worldwide. Our zirconia and porcelain crowns are covered by a seven-year clinic guarantee.

Clinic-verified information · Topic: General

05Is treatment painful? Will I need sedation or anaesthetic?

TR: Tedavi ağrılı mı? Sedasyon veya anestezi gerekir mi?

Because every procedure is carried out under local anaesthetic, you won't feel any pain during treatment. For patients who feel anxious about treatment, laughing-gas sedation is also available at no extra charge.

Clinic-verified information · Topic: Treatments

06How do dental prices in Turkey compare with the UK, Germany or the US?

TR: Türkiye diş fiyatları İngiltere, Almanya veya ABD ile nasıl kıyaslanır?

Dental treatment in Turkey is typically 60–75% more affordable than equivalent procedures in Western Europe or North America, with no compromise on materials, technology or clinical standards.

Clinic-verified information · Topic: General

07Can I combine dental treatment with a holiday in Antalya?

TR: Diş tedavisini Antalya tatiliyle birleştirebilir miyim?

Absolutely — and many of our patients do exactly that. Treatment sessions usually take only a few hours a day, leaving your mornings or afternoons free to explore Antalya.
