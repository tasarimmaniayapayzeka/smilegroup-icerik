Privacy and Cookie Policy

At Smile Group, protecting the privacy of the patients and users who visit our site is our most fundamental principle.

Data Security

All contact forms and data transmission processes on our site are protected with up-to-date encryption standards (SSL certificates). Your information is hosted on secure servers, and periodic penetration tests together with administrative and technical security measures are applied to prevent unauthorised access to our systems.

Use of Cookies

TR: Çerez (Cookie) Kullanımı

Our website uses cookies to deliver the system's core functions, carry out performance analysis and offer you a better user experience. By using our site, you are deemed to have accepted the use of strictly necessary cookies. You can manage or refuse your cookie preferences at any time by changing your browser settings.
