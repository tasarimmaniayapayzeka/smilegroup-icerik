Social Responsibility

TR: Sosyal Sorumluluklarımız

The social responsibility projects we run to contribute to the health of the wider community.

Smile Ambassadors

Healthy Habits Start at an Early Age

TR: Sağlıklı Alışkanlıklar Küçük Yaşlarda Başlar (rozet: GÜLÜŞ ELÇİLERİ)

We organise educational activities in schools to help children become more aware of their oral and dental health. Through fun, hands-on activities and interactive sessions, we aim to help them build the right oral care habits from an early age.

Silent Smiles

Nothing Should Stand in the Way of a Healthy Smile

TR: Sağlıklı Bir Gülüşün Önünde Engel Olmamalı (rozet: SESSİZ GÜLÜŞLER)

We place great importance on awareness work so that everyone can access oral and dental care on an equal footing. Through training sessions, information meetings and advisory events for individuals with special needs and their families, we support a more inclusive approach to healthcare.

Smile Bursary

One Smile, One Future

TR: Bir Gülüş, Bir Gelecek (rozet: GÜLÜŞ BURSU)

At Smile Group, we believe in the difference oral and dental health makes to quality of life. Through our Smile Bursary programme, we aim to help young people in need of treatment regain their oral health.

Run to a quota set each year, the programme is designed to help young people step into a healthier, more confident and more hopeful future with a smile.

Smiles That Take Root

Healthy Smiles, a Greener Future

TR: Sağlıklı Gülüşler, Daha Yeşil Bir Gelecek (rozet: KÖK SALAN GÜLÜŞLER)

By contributing to tree-planting projects carried out on behalf of our patients undergoing implant treatment, we aim to make sure every new beginning also adds value to nature. We believe a healthy smile can bring hope not only to individuals, but to the future as well.
