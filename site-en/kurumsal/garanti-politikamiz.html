Our Warranty Policy

The scope and conditions of the warranty we offer on the treatments we provide.

WARRANTY POLICY

TR: GARANTİ POLİTİKASI

Last Updated: 22.07.2026

As Smilegroup Ağız ve Diş Sağlığı Polikliniği (Smilegroup Oral and Dental Health Polyclinic), we place quality, trust and patient satisfaction at the heart of every treatment process we offer our patients. Our treatments are carried out in line with current scientific approaches, using materials and methods that meet international standards.

This Warranty Policy has been prepared to explain the general warranty conditions that apply to the treatments carried out at our clinic.

1. Scope of the Warranty

TR: 1. Garanti Kapsamı

The materials and procedures used in the treatments carried out at our clinic are assessed in line with the manufacturers' warranties and medical standards.

The warranty may cover the following treatments.

  • Dental implant procedures
  • Zirconia and porcelain crowns
  • Veneer (porcelain laminate) procedures
  • Bridge prostheses
  • Filling procedures
  • Removable dentures
  • Other prosthetic restorations

The scope of the warranty for each treatment may vary depending on the material used, the treatment plan and the manufacturer's warranty conditions.

2. Warranty Conditions

TR: 2. Garanti Şartları

For the warranty to remain valid, the following conditions must be met.

  • The patient follows the recommended treatment plan
  • Oral and dental care is carried out regularly
  • The check-up appointments recommended by the dentist are attended regularly
  • Recommended protective appliances, such as a night guard, are used (where deemed necessary)
  • Habits that could damage the restorations are avoided

3. Circumstances Not Covered by the Warranty

TR: 3. Garanti Kapsamı Dışındaki Durumlar

The following circumstances are not considered to fall within the scope of the warranty.

  • Missed or neglected regular check-ups
  • Inadequate oral hygiene
  • Fractures or cracks caused by tooth clenching (bruxism)
  • Damage caused by accident, impact or trauma
  • Procedures carried out at other healthcare institutions
  • Complications that may arise from smoking or a lack of oral care
  • Problems arising from the patient not following the dentist's recommendations
  • Conditions arising from natural bone loss or the biological healing process

4. Check-Up Examinations

TR: 4. Kontrol Muayeneleri

Regular check-up examinations after treatment are of great importance for the long-term success of the treatment.

We advise our patients to attend a routine dental check-up at least twice a year.

5. Assessment of the Warranty Process

TR: 5. Garanti Sürecinin Değerlendirilmesi

If a situation arises that may fall within the scope of the warranty, the restoration or treatment concerned is first examined clinically by our dentists.

If this assessment establishes that the problem is covered by the warranty, the necessary corrective procedure is planned.

6. Important Information

TR: 6. Önemli Bilgilendirme

Dental treatments can produce different results depending on each patient's oral structure, bone level, general health and healing process.

For this reason, no lifetime or unconditional warranty is given for any treatment.

Our clinic assesses every case individually, in line with patient safety and the principles of scientific ethics.

7. Contact

TR: 7. İletişim

You can contact us for detailed information about our warranty policy or to request support regarding your treatment.
