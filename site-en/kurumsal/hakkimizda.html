Why Smilegroup?

TR: NEDEN SMILEGROUP?
  • We are a clinic that provides genuine healthcare to its patients.
  • We welcome every patient with the highest standard of service in mind.
  • We strive to achieve 100% happiness and satisfaction for our patients.
  • When our patients have a problem with their teeth, they come to us without a moment's worry — because everything we do is entirely health-led, with no commercial agenda.
  • After every appointment, we ask our patients to review their visit that day, because satisfaction and a high standard of service matter to us.
  • Because Smilegroup is built on a model that places its dentists at the centre, it has never entered into any outside business partnership. That is why our patients continue to choose us, year after year.
  • Our clinic is equipped with a state-of-the-art 3D tomography scanner, so we never refer you to another centre for your scans.
  • We also have a 3D intraoral scanner, which means your crowns are designed entirely in a digital environment.
  • For the anaesthetic used during treatment, we use digital anaesthesia rather than conventional syringe needles — so any sensation of pain is kept to a minimum even while you're being numbed.
  • In root canal treatment sessions, we use laser-assisted systems and sterile endodontic instruments to achieve results to international standards.
  • Inside the clinic there is a comfortable lounge area and coffee bar where you can relax while waiting for your appointment.
  • SmileGroup also thinks of your finances, offering instalment options so that treatment doesn't strain your budget.
